# Basic Playback

The main window's control bar, and playback commands such as open, seek and speed.

## Features

- **Control bar**: seek bar, play/pause, stop, previous and next (the playlist's previous and next item, as PgUp/PgDn; off at the ends of the list, from 0.2.14), open, fullscreen (hidden when the bar is narrow), mute and volume, and at the right end the player's strip of command buttons (other plugins' buttons and Toggle Sidebar; it narrows first); click the current time to switch between elapsed and remaining time. Button tooltips show the current shortcut.
- **Volume and mute are remembered**: the last volume and mute (set with the bar, the keys or anything else) come back when the player starts again.
- **Open**: open a file, open a link (can fill in a link from the clipboard automatically), reopen the current or last played file; copy the file name or path.
- **Playback panel (0.2.18)**: Playback… (Alt+4) opens it in the adjust column beside the video. a speed ruler with every preset as a labelled tick (0.25 · 0.5 · 1× · 1.25 · 1.5 · 2 · 4, 1× in the centre): click a label, drag (snaps at the ticks, 0.05 steps between them), scroll ±0.1, double-click for 1×, or click the speed to type any speed from 0.25× to 4×; a time field to jump to (G focuses it) and Jump to Start; frame and keyframe steps; the four seek steps with their keys on both sides (click a value to type a new one, double-click for its default) and "Jump to keyframes only". The keys shown are the ones bound now.
- **Seek**: four seek steps (change them in the Playback panel), previous/next keyframe, jump to the start, jump to a given time.
- **Jump to keyframes only (0.2.1; the seek bar too from 0.2.3)**: on by default, set in the Playback panel. The four seek steps (←/→ and the Ctrl, Shift and Ctrl+Alt steps) then land on a keyframe near the target, forward on or after it and back on or before it, so a press never freezes the picture on files with few keyframes, and each press always moves. The on-screen message shows where the seek landed. From 0.2.3 the seek bar follows the same switch: a click or a drag lands on the keyframe at or before the pointer, so the picture appears within about 0.1 s on any file and a drag keeps updating on files with keyframes far apart (4K recordings with a keyframe every 10 s froze before), at the price of landing up to one keyframe interval before the pointer; the mode is decided at the press and kept for the whole gesture, and a click is one seek. The message shown at release names the point you let go of; the time label shows where the seek landed. Turn it off for exact seeks everywhere, as before. Jump to Time, Jump to Start, chapters, bookmarks and the A-B loop always seek exactly. Needs Vestra Player 0.5.3 or later.
- **Speed**: 0.5×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2× and any speed from 0.25× to 4×.
- **Seek bar hover**: shows the time or a thumbnail at the pointer (thumbnails need ffmpeg).
- **Seek bar thumbnails (0.2.0)**: on by default for local video files, shown on hover and while dragging the seek bar. A few seconds after a file starts playing, a set of thumbnails (up to 200) is made in the background; until then the nearest finished thumbnail is shown. Opening a file is never slowed down by this: nothing runs before the first frame is on screen, at most two ffmpeg processes run at a time, and they pause while you drag or seek. While they are being made, the pictures under a resting pointer fill in every 2.5 s or so (0.2.3; the player decodes every new sheet on its UI thread, so the bar hands them over less often while you look at it). Thumbnails are kept for next time (up to 200 MB in the plugin's data folder, oldest first out). Turn them off with Show Thumbnail on Seek Bar Hover. Network streams show the time only for now.
- **Exact thumbnails for short files (0.2.2)**: a file that is cheap enough to decode whole gets one thumbnail every half second (or 200 over its length, or one per frame for slideshow-like files, whichever is coarser), each the frame on screen at that time, so a phone clip with a keyframe every 5–10 s no longer shows the same picture over a whole stretch of the bar. "Cheap enough" is a budget, not a length: the estimated decoding time — width × height × frame rate × duration at 4 ns per pixel — must stay within about 15 s, which is what the 200 keyframe thumbnails of a long file already cost. That is a 1080p30 file up to 60 s, 1080p60 up to 30 s, 720p30 up to about 2 min; 4K files and longer files keep keyframe thumbnails as before. The pass decodes in software (the computer's video decoder is left to the playing video: sharing it was measured to stall 4K playback) in parts that begin at the file's keyframes, one thread each, two at a time, so nothing is decoded twice and the first thumbnails appear after about two seconds; the same drag and seek pauses apply. If a part takes more than twice as long as estimated, the computer is slower or busier than assumed, and the file gets keyframe thumbnails instead; that is remembered, so the next time the file opens it is not tried again.
- **No screen saver while playing**.

## Where to find it

- Right-click menu: Open File… (Ctrl+O, F3), Open ▸ Open URL… (Ctrl+U).
- Right-click menu: Playback… (Alt+4), the Playback panel.
- Command palette (Ctrl+Shift+P), with their keys: File: Reopen Current/Last File (Ctrl+Y); Copy to Clipboard: File Name, Folder Path, Full Path; Seek: Previous/Next Keyframe (Ctrl+Shift+←/→), Back/Forward 30 s (Ctrl+←/→), 60 s (Shift+←/→), 300 s (Ctrl+Alt+←/→), Jump to Time… (G), Jump to Start (Backspace), Seek Steps…; Speed: 0.5×, 1.5×, 2×, Custom…; Playback: Disable Screen Saver While Playing, Show Time / Thumbnail on Seek Bar Hover.

Basic actions such as play/pause, back/forward 5 seconds, volume and mute are provided by the player and keep working when this plugin is turned off (without it, the volume is not remembered).
