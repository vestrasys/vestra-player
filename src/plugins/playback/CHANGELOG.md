# Changelog

## 0.2.18

Who must change: nobody. Needs player 0.7.6 with one panel per slot (SDK 1.6.15); `minPlayerVersion` stays 0.7.6 (players before the multi-panel change refuse the second panel, and the plugin does not start there). What users see: **Playback…** (Alt+4) in the right-click menu opens the Playback panel in the adjust column beside the video; pressing it again while the panel is in front closes the column (menu redesign step 7, mockup 03 left).

- **Speed**: − / the speed / + (the player's Slower 0.1 and Faster 0.1, X and C), and 0.5× / 1× (Z) / 1.25× / 1.5× / 2×, the current one outlined. A click on the speed types any speed from 0.25× to 4× (Enter applies; Esc or leaving the field keeps it), which replaces the custom speed window. **Jump**: a time field (G focuses it; `h:mm:ss.mmm`, leading parts optional) with Go, which replaces the Jump to Time window, and Jump to Start (Backspace). **Step**: Frame ◀ ▶ (the player's D and F) and Keyframe ◀ ▶ (Ctrl+Shift+← →), greyed with the reason. **Seek steps**: the four steps, each with its keys on both sides; a click on a value types a new one (0.1–3600 s, one decimal), a double click restores its default, and "Jump to keyframes only" sits under them. A step applies when typed: the player's ← / → step at once, the Ctrl, Shift and Ctrl+Alt commands with their new titles. This replaces the Seek Steps window (no OK / Cancel any more).
- A refused value (a time past the end, a speed out of range, a step out of range) keeps its field open with an accent ring, and the reason shows on the video; leaving the field drops the mark.
- Key caps beside the controls and in the tooltips are the keys bound now: this plugin's from `plugin.commands.keys`, the player's own actions (X, C, Z, D, F, ← →) from the binding table (`plugin.bindings`). A key the user clears shows no cap.
- One column, every label one line (elided, its whole text in a tooltip); the elements are always there and only change their values. A text field with the keyboard keeps the fullscreen column out (`setInteraction({ focused })`).
- Menus: Playback ▸ Speed and Playback ▸ Seek are gone, and so are Jump to Time… and Seek Steps… in Playback. Their commands keep their ids and keys and are in the command palette as "Seek: …" and "Speed: …", with the Playback panel as their place (`home: "panel"`): Previous / Next Keyframe, Jump to Time… (G: the panel with its time field focused), Jump to Start, Seek Steps… (the panel at its Seek steps), 0.5×, 1.5×, 2×, Custom… (the panel with the speed open for typing) and the Ctrl, Shift and Ctrl+Alt seek steps. The player's own seek, frame and speed actions left those submenus in the same release. Disable Screen Saver While Playing and the two seek-bar hover options stay in Playback for now (they move to Options with the menu redesign's last step).
- The windows `playback-jump`, `playback-rate` and `playback-time-jump` (`ui/prompt.slint`, `ui/time-jump.slint`) are removed; their texts are obsolete in the catalogs. New texts are translated in all six languages (machine translations, `x-machine`).

## 0.2.17

Who must change: nobody. Needs player 0.7.6 (`minPlayerVersion` already 0.7.6). What users see: the command buttons at the right end of the bar (other plugins' buttons, `»`, the command palette 🔍 and Toggle Sidebar) are now placed by the player (menu redesign step 3a, SDK 1.6.8); the bar keeps its own controls left of them and never lets them cover the time.

- The bar no longer places the deprecated `BarButtons` strip. It imports `BarButtonsHost` and reports what its button row needs as `content-min-width` (its buttons, the 10px gap, the time and 8px to spare); the player gives its strip the rest of the width. `content-min-width` does not read the strip's width.
- It also reports `content-full-width`, its row with every control shown: the player gives plugin buttons only the width beyond it, so while the window narrows every plugin button goes to `»` before the bar drops a control of its own, and none comes back.
- UI-BAR-10: Fullscreen shows while it fits beside the player's own cells (`BarButtonsHost.fixed`, 41px each): from 6 × 41 + 10 + time + 8 + fixed × 41 + 4 px, about 435px with 🔍 and Sidebar and an 85px time (was 394px beside the sidebar toggle alone).
- Narrow windows (responsive-player practice): after Fullscreen the bar drops its less important controls one step at a time, so the time stays clear of the player's cells: **Stop** below about 394px, **Previous / Next** below about 353px, **Open** below about 271px (85px time, 🔍 and Sidebar; each threshold is measured like Fullscreen's). Play / Pause always shows. `content-min-width` shrinks with them. A dropped button is hidden with no width and is not actionable from the accessibility tree; its command, menu item and key are unchanged.
- Timeline ranges (SDK 1.6.9, player 0.7.6): the seek bar draws every plugin's `timeline.ranges` as semi-transparent bands where the mark ticks are, over the line and under the A-B loop and the ticks: `region` in the dim text colour, `selection` (such as the clip Capture will export) in the accent colour. Each style is one Path of whole physical px rectangles in the ticks' view box (no element per range); overlapping ranges of a style are drawn once. They follow every change, the window's width and scale, and go with the source. A range's title is not shown on hover yet.

## 0.2.12

- Seek-bar preview cache: a sprite's `index.json` is written atomically, as a temporary file in the same folder (`index.json.<pid>.<n>.tmp`) renamed over it. Before, it was written in place, so another source's cache clean-up could read half of it, take the folder for a broken one and delete it (the sprite was made again on the next open). A rename refused for a moment on Windows (a reader such as a clean-up, a virus scanner or the indexer has the file open) is tried again a few times; a write that fails removes its temporary file. Readers only read `index.json`. A clean-up no longer deletes a folder whose index cannot be read just then (a sharing violation, too many open files) or that has a temporary index less than a minute old (a write in progress); one left over from a crash is removed once it is older.
- Stopping the plugin waits, best effort, for the sprite's pending disk work (index writes, sheet writes, cache clean-ups), 250 ms at most (the player gives a stopping plugin 300 ms in the shared runner), so as a rule nothing is written into or deleted from the data directory after the plugin stopped; on a disk slower than that a write can still land later. A new source's cache clean-up likewise waits (as long at most) for the previous source's index writes, and it keeps, as they are when it runs, the current source's folder (which may have begun meanwhile, without an index yet) and the folders of ended sources whose index writes have not landed.
- Nobody needs to change anything.

## 0.2.11

- Seek-bar preview: newly made thumbnails reach the disk (and the bar) as soon as their cells land, also when the disk is slow. Before, a sheet write counted as busy until its `index.json` was saved too; a run of cells that landed meanwhile freed its ffmpeg slot to the next run instead of the write, so the new sheet (and its hand-over to the bar) waited until another run ended: a few seconds for keyframe cells, up to a whole part in exact mode. The index is now saved after the write without holding it up; index saves of one sprite run in order, so the newest always lands last.
- When the exact pass falls back to keyframes, the keyframe sprite starts once the exact folder's index (`keyframesOnly`) is on disk. Before, the new sprite's cache clean-up could run while that index was still being written, see the exact folder without a valid index and delete it, so the next open tried (and failed) the exact pass again.
- The seek band reaches 4px higher (user request): hovering (time tip, mark tip, preview thumbnail), pressing and dragging also work in the 4px just above the bar's top edge, over the bottom of the video, as anywhere else in the seek row; nothing changes below the row. The look is unchanged (line, knob, ticks, and the tip 4px above the line). In those 4px a click no longer reaches the video (no play/pause, double-click fullscreen, window drag or right-click menu there); the wheel still does. Unless nothing can be seeked: then the band takes no input and the video gets it as before. While the pointer is on the band the bar holds an interaction lock (`popupOpen`), so the fullscreen bar stays up in those 4px, where the player sees the pointer over neither the bar nor the video.
- Nobody needs to change anything.

## 0.2.8

- Mark ticks are whole physical pixels at every scale factor (125%, 150%, 175%, as well as 100% and 200%): at a fractional scale 0.2.7's 1px stroke was 1.5 physical px wide and still left a half-transparent column. The ticks are now drawn in physical pixels: the bar reports the seek line's span in whole physical px (`span-changed`, also when the scale factor changes), the plugin builds the commands at whole physical x in a viewbox that wide, and the Paths have a 1 physical px stroke (`1phx`) and their left edge and top on a physical pixel (from the seek bar's `absolute-position`). Each tick is one physical pixel wide and about 6 logical px tall in whole pixel rows; its column is within half a physical pixel of where the logical layout puts it.
- A window resize no longer rebuilds and sends the three tick strings every frame: after the first report, span reports take effect once they pause for 100 ms (the last wins); meanwhile the ticks stretch with the line as before.
- Above 4096 marks, the user's bookmarks are kept first (all of them, or 4096 picked evenly if they alone are more) and the chapters and markers are picked evenly into the room left, all in time order.

## 0.2.7

- The seek line's mark ticks are whole pixels again, as before 0.2.6: each 1px tick fills exactly the pixel column the marks had then (`floor(4px + at × span)`) instead of being antialiased across two half-transparent columns. The bar reports the seek line's span to the plugin at start and when its width changes (`span-changed`); the plugin builds each kind's commands with the tick at the column's whole-pixel x in a viewbox as wide as that span (`tick-span`), drawn at scale 1, where Slint's half-stroke inset centres the stroke on the column. During a resize the ticks stretch to the new width until the plugin's update arrives. Ticks show once the bar has reported its span.
- More marks than the 4096 the bar shows are now picked evenly in time order (by index, the first and the last kept) instead of dropping everything after the 4096th, so a long file's later marks still show and the hover search stays sorted.
- The hover tips' text budget counts a control character (or a lone surrogate) as the 6 bytes of its JSON escape (`\u00XX`), not 3. Each kind's tick string also has a 60 KiB cap as a safety net under the player's 64 KiB per string (not reached in practice: one tick per mark, at most 4096).
- The bottom bar's volume slider stays out of the Tab chain (`focusable: false` on the library `Slider`). The library slider now takes keyboard focus, so with Tab unbound (or "Ignore default shortcuts" on) Tab could move into it and the arrow keys then changed the volume instead of seeking. Dragging and clicking it work as before. The seek line is the bar's own `SeekBar` (a `TouchArea`, no `FocusScope`), which never took keyboard focus.
- Nobody needs to change anything.

## 0.2.3

- The seek bar follows "Jump to keyframes only" (user ruling 2026-10-27): with the switch on (the default), a click or a drag on the bar seeks by keyframes, like the arrow keys, so the picture appears within about 0.1 s on any file and a drag keeps updating on files with keyframes far apart, up to one keyframe interval before the pointer; off, the bar seeks exactly as before. The mode is decided when the pointer is pressed and kept for the whole gesture, with no second exact seek on release. A click sends one seek: the release on the same spot is not sent again. The on-screen message at release ("Seek: 00:59 / 01:40") names the point you let go of; in keyframe mode the picture then lands on the keyframe at or before it (00:50, say), which the bar's time label shows once it lands (the arrow keys' message names the landed time because the player writes it; a plugin's seek is acknowledged when it is accepted, before it lands). Jump to Time, Jump to Start, chapters, bookmarks and the A-B loop still seek exactly. Needs Vestra Player 0.5.3 or later (`Scrub.seek(seconds, { keyframes })`, SDK 1.5.22).
- The hover tip and its thumbnail are permanent elements of the bar shown with `visible:` instead of being created on every pointer enter and whenever the cell crossed from one sheet to the other: each creation uploaded the 1600×900 sheet texture again (about 4 ms on the player's UI thread) and rebuilt the window's accessibility tree.
- While thumbnails are being made and the pointer is on the bar, changed sheets are handed to the player at most every 2.5 s instead of every second (the player decodes each sheet on its UI thread, about 12 ms per 1600×900 JPEG), and a sheet without new cells keeps its reference, so it is not decoded again. Sheets still reach the disk every second, nothing is held back while nobody points, and the pointer's arrival shows the newest sheets at once.

## 0.2.2

- Seek bar thumbnails are exact for short files: when decoding the whole file is estimated to take no more than about 15 s (width × height × frame rate × duration at 4 ns per pixel), the thumbnails are the frames on screen every half second (at most 200, never finer than a frame), instead of the keyframe at or before each slot; a span without frames (a slideshow, a still stretch of a screen recording) repeats the frame still on screen. Measured on 1080p30 H.264 that is any file up to 60 s, on 1080p60 up to 30 s; 4K files and longer files keep the keyframe thumbnails of 0.2.0. The pass decodes in software only (ffmpeg's hardware decoding would share the video engine with the playing video; measured on 4K60 HEVC it stalled playback to 18–31 fps), in parts that start at the file's keyframes (ffprobe lists them), one thread each, two at a time, with the first thumbnails after about two seconds. A pass that fails, or a part that takes more than twice its estimate, falls back to keyframe thumbnails, and the file's cache entry remembers it so later opens do not try again. Exact sprites are cached under their own key; keyframe sprites made by 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 are kept.
