# Changelog

## 0.2.5

- The bottom bar places the player's strip of command buttons (`BarButtons` from `@vestra/bar-buttons.slint`, player 0.5.10) at the right end of the button row: the buttons other plugins give their commands (the AI workshop, the store…), left to right by their order, and the player's own **Toggle Sidebar** rightmost. The bar's own 23×22 playlist toggle is gone: the strip's toggle is the player's command `host/sidebar`, with the sidebar's state (accent while on, half opacity while on but hidden for want of room) and its own tooltip and key, so the bar no longer reads or sets the sidebar position and sends no sidebar data.
- Narrow windows (UI-BAR-10): the time is never cut. The strip gets only the width the six buttons, the 10px gap, the time and 8px to spare leave (less its 4px right margin) and narrows first, one 41px cell at a time (the player picks which buttons go; its toggle stays longest). The Fullscreen button shows while the toggle's cell still fits beside it, from 6 × 41 + 10 + time + 8 + 41 + 4 px (about 394 px for `0:00:00 / 0:00:00`; 380 px with the old toggle); below that it hides, then the toggle once five buttons, the time and its cell no longer fit (about 353 px), and below a bar width of 320 px the strip gets no room.
- Needs Vestra Player 0.5.10 or later (`@vestra/bar-buttons.slint`, SDK 1.5.29).

## 0.2.4

- A Fullscreen button on the bottom bar (user request, UI-BAR-05), right of Open and left of the time, the same 41×40 icon button as the others: a click toggles the main window's fullscreen exactly like Enter / F11 (`host.run("fullscreen")`), in when windowed, out when fullscreen. Its icon follows the window (`window.state`): `fullscreen` when windowed, `exit-fullscreen` in fullscreen; the tooltip reads "Fullscreen" or "Leave fullscreen" with the key bound to Toggle Fullscreen (Enter by default), translated like the other tooltips. The time keeps priority (UI-BAR-10): the button is the first thing hidden when the bar is too narrow, below 6 × 41 + 10 + the time label's width + 8 + 23 + 8 px, where the time's width is measured in the bar's font, not a constant (about 380 px for `0:00:00 / 0:00:00`), at its widest for the file (the elapsed part as wide as "-" and the total), so the button does not come and go while the time runs or when it switches to the remaining time. Hidden is a permanent element with `visible: false`, no width and disabled (not actionable from the accessibility tree), never `if`. Needs Vestra Player 0.5.2 or later (`host.run("fullscreen")`, SDK 1.5.21).

## 0.2.3

- The seek bar follows "Jump to keyframes only" (user ruling 2026-10-27): with the switch on (the default), a click or a drag on the bar seeks by keyframes, like the arrow keys, so the picture appears within about 0.1 s on any file and a drag keeps updating on files with keyframes far apart, up to one keyframe interval before the pointer; off, the bar seeks exactly as before. The mode is decided when the pointer is pressed and kept for the whole gesture, with no second exact seek on release. A click sends one seek: the release on the same spot is not sent again. The on-screen message at release ("Seek: 00:59 / 01:40") names the point you let go of; in keyframe mode the picture then lands on the keyframe at or before it (00:50, say), which the bar's time label shows once it lands (the arrow keys' message names the landed time because the player writes it; a plugin's seek is acknowledged when it is accepted, before it lands). Jump to Time, Jump to Start, chapters, bookmarks and the A-B loop still seek exactly. Needs Vestra Player 0.5.3 or later (`Scrub.seek(seconds, { keyframes })`, SDK 1.5.22).
- The hover tip and its thumbnail are permanent elements of the bar shown with `visible:` instead of being created on every pointer enter and whenever the cell crossed from one sheet to the other: each creation uploaded the 1600×900 sheet texture again (about 4 ms on the player's UI thread) and rebuilt the window's accessibility tree.
- While thumbnails are being made and the pointer is on the bar, changed sheets are handed to the player at most every 2.5 s instead of every second (the player decodes each sheet on its UI thread, about 12 ms per 1600×900 JPEG), and a sheet without new cells keeps its reference, so it is not decoded again. Sheets still reach the disk every second, nothing is held back while nobody points, and the pointer's arrival shows the newest sheets at once.

## 0.2.2

- Seek bar thumbnails are exact for short files: when decoding the whole file is estimated to take no more than about 15 s (width × height × frame rate × duration at 4 ns per pixel), the thumbnails are the frames on screen every half second (at most 200, never finer than a frame), instead of the keyframe at or before each slot; a span without frames (a slideshow, a still stretch of a screen recording) repeats the frame still on screen. Measured on 1080p30 H.264 that is any file up to 60 s, on 1080p60 up to 30 s; 4K files and longer files keep the keyframe thumbnails of 0.2.0. The pass decodes in software only (ffmpeg's hardware decoding would share the video engine with the playing video; measured on 4K60 HEVC it stalled playback to 18–31 fps), in parts that start at the file's keyframes (ffprobe lists them), one thread each, two at a time, with the first thumbnails after about two seconds. A pass that fails, or a part that takes more than twice its estimate, falls back to keyframe thumbnails, and the file's cache entry remembers it so later opens do not try again. Exact sprites are cached under their own key; keyframe sprites made by 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 are kept.

## 0.2.1

- Jump to keyframes only (on by default, set in Seek Steps…): the four seek steps land on a keyframe near the target. Needs Vestra Player 0.5.1 or later.

## 0.2.0

- Seek bar thumbnails: a keyframe sprite of each local video file, made in the background after the first frame and shown on hover and while dragging; on by default; cached across sessions.
