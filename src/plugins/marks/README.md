# Chapters and Bookmarks

Jump between chapters and bookmarks.

## Features

- **Chapters**: lists the media's own chapters; click one to jump to it, or step to the previous or next chapter.
- **Bookmarks**: add bookmarks to each file; search, rename, retime and remove them in the list window. When the file has changed, the window warns that bookmark times may be off.
- **Seek-bar marks**: shows chapters and bookmarks as marks on the seek bar.

A-B Loop moved to the Playback panel (Basic Playback, 0.1.8): its keys (`[`, `]`, `\` and the rest) and its "A-B Loop: …" and "Loop Count: …" commands are the same there.

## Where to find it

Context menu: Chapter and Bookmark List… (H), at the top level after Playlist.

Command palette (Ctrl+Shift+P), with their keys, "Chapters and Bookmarks: …":

- Add Bookmark (P)
- Previous Bookmark or Chapter, Next Bookmark or Chapter (Shift+PgUp, Shift+PgDn)
- Previous Chapter, Next Chapter (Ctrl+PgUp, Ctrl+PgDn)
- the chapters and bookmarks of the open file
- Show Marks on Seek Bar

## Limits

- The command palette lists at most 50 chapters and 50 bookmarks; see the rest in the list window.
