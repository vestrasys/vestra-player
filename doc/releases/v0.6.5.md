# Vestra Player 0.6.5 (unsigned test build)

Internal test build, not signed or notarized. A small follow-up to 0.6.4: on Windows, recovering from a lost graphics device no longer ends the old player abnormally.

## Playback and stability

- **A lost graphics device is recovered from cleanly** (Windows). After the graphics device was lost (or the GPU watchdog stopped a stalled renderer), the old player could crash while drawing its last frames on the lost device and skip its normal shutdown: plugins were not told the player quit (they read `host-lost`), and the new player, which waits for the old one to end, started later. The old player now finishes its normal shutdown, plugins are told `quit`, and the new player starts sooner (about 2 seconds after the loss in our test, 7 before). `host.log` and `restart.log` say "the event loop panicked after the GPU device loss" after the `panic:` line that names what failed.

## Lazy activation (experimental, off by default)

- **A parked plugin's menus keep their order.** With lazy activation on (`VESTRA_LAZY=1`), a plugin parked from its recording at start got its commands back in the order of their ids: its menu items of equal order could change places, and so could which of its buttons shows in the bottom bar. A recording now keeps the order the plugin registered them in.

## Installing an unsigned build

- **macOS** 15 or later, Apple silicon: open the DMG and drag Vestra Player into Applications. Gatekeeper blocks the first launch: open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Vestra Player.app"`. Files downloaded with `gh release download` are not quarantined.
- **Windows** 10/11 x64: run `VestraPlayer-0.6.5-windows-x64-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, choose **More info ▸ Run anyway**. It installs to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Vestra Player` without administrator rights. Uninstalling keeps your plugins and settings in `%APPDATA%\Vestra` unless you choose otherwise. Or unzip the portable zip and run `vestra-host.exe` (the portable build adds no Open with entries).

## Known issues

- Check for Updates… reports "no release information was found (HTTP 404)" until a release is published (not a draft) in a public repository; internal test builds cannot find each other.
- Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish are machine translations, reviewed once by an AI reviewer; a human review is still to come. Corrections are welcome.
- Numbers use "." as the decimal separator in every language (1.5×, 2.0 dB).
- This build is not signed or notarized (see above).
- macOS builds are Apple silicon only. Intel Macs and macOS 14 are not supported yet.
- Picture-in-picture is macOS only. GPU rendering on Windows is experimental and off by default.
