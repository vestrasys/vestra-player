# Vestra Player 0.7.5 (unsigned test build)

Internal test build, not signed or notarized. The two arrow buttons in the bottom bar now go to the previous and next item of the playlist.

## Bottom bar

- **« and » are now Previous and Next.** They play the previous or next item of the playlist, the same as Playback ▸ Previous / Next, PgUp / PgDn and the mouse's side buttons. Before, a click skipped 5 seconds back or forward and a right-click went to the previous or next item. They show previous/next icons, and their tooltips name the key ("Previous (PgUp)", "Next (PgDn)").
- **They follow the playlist**: Previous is greyed out on the first item and Next on the last one; with Repeat All both stay available. Without a playlist both are greyed out.
- **Seeking with the keyboard is unchanged**: ← and → (with Ctrl, Shift or Ctrl+Alt for other steps) and the seek bar work as before.

## Installing an unsigned build

- **macOS** 15 or later, Apple silicon: open the DMG and drag Vestra Player into Applications. Gatekeeper blocks the first launch: open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Vestra Player.app"`. Files downloaded with `gh release download` are not quarantined.
- **Windows** 10/11 x64: run `VestraPlayer-0.7.5-windows-x64-setup.exe`. If SmartScreen warns, choose **More info ▸ Run anyway**. It installs to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Vestra Player` without administrator rights. Uninstalling keeps your plugins and settings in `%APPDATA%\Vestra` unless you choose otherwise. Or unzip the portable zip and run `vestra-host.exe` (the portable build adds no Open with entries).

## Known issues

- The tab you are on keeps its plugin running even while the sidebar is hidden.
- When the set of prepared tabs changes, a detached sidebar window may redraw once.
- Check for Updates… reports "no release information was found (HTTP 404)" until a release is published (not a draft) in a public repository; internal test builds cannot find each other.
- Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish are machine translations, reviewed once by an AI reviewer; a human review is still to come. Corrections are welcome.
- Numbers use "." as the decimal separator in every language (1.5×, 2.0 dB).
- This build is not signed or notarized (see above).
- macOS builds are Apple silicon only. Intel Macs and macOS 14 are not supported yet.
- Picture-in-picture is macOS only. GPU rendering on Windows is experimental and off by default.
